Yes. If you say you’re starting from zero, your prompt tells Claude to explain everything step by step, one command at a time.

Why so many questions?

Three questions can’t tell us who you are. Real context is what filters out ideas that aren’t for you.

We can’t promise income. We give you the idea, the build and the plan. Results depend on your work and your market.
